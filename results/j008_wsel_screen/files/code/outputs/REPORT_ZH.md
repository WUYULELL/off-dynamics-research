# j008_wsel_screen：任务层面选择源数据权重（模型选权重 SEL；固定权重 W25、W50）筛查

种子 [3116, 3117, 3118, 3119, 3120]（新种子）；三个任务、四个源权重 w ∈ {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1} 都在同一张卡、同一批种子下训练，w=0 即 BC，w=1 即 BC-混合。主指标 Last5（最后五个评估点平均，每点 10 回合），分数按 ODRL 参考回报归一化。SEL 不训练新策略：每个（任务、种子）用目标动力学模型（源数据预训练 + 目标数据微调的 5 个模型）从重置分布的起点模拟四个策略，选模拟回报最高的那个 w，报告它在真实环境里的 Last5。

## 预设规则（提交前写进 research/PLAN.md）

- metric：Last5 = mean of the last five evaluation points, 10 episodes each (V21/j004-j007 metric)
- SEL：per (task, seed), the Last5 of the policy whose weight the model-based score chose; no policy is retrained and no return is used in the choice
- strong_control：per task, the higher Last5 mean of BC (W0) and BC-mix (W100) over seeds 3116-3120; T (target-only IQL) is not run (budget: one GPU) - it was below the strong control on all three tasks in j004, j005 and j006
- screen_effective：candidate minus strong control, paired by seed: mean > 0 and mean > 1 * paired SE
- supported：for a candidate (SEL, W25, W50): screen-effective on at least 2 of the 3 tasks -> confirmation with 5 never-used seeds on those tasks (T included there)
- rejected：paired mean difference <= 0 on at least 2 of the 3 tasks
- otherwise：uncertain; not advanced to confirmation
- secondary：registered, not counted by the loop: SEL is robust if on every task SEL minus strong control has paired mean >= -1 * paired SE; also reported: SEL minus BC-mix on walker medium
- descriptive：2 * SE criterion, Spearman correlation between model score and true Last5 over the four weights per (task, seed), chosen vs best weight, regret, SEL with the 200-step score, per-seed oracle, model validation error, BC-mix minus BC
- note：Rules are fixed before results; not changed after results are seen. Three candidates are compared; this is stated in the report. If any run fails, no verdict.

- SEL：**否定**（筛查有效的任务：无；均值差 ≤ 0 的任务：hopper medium、walker medium、walker hard）
- W25：**否定**（筛查有效的任务：无；均值差 ≤ 0 的任务：hopper medium、walker medium、walker hard）
- W50：**否定**（筛查有效的任务：无；均值差 ≤ 0 的任务：hopper medium、walker medium、walker hard）
